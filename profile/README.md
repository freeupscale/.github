<h3 align="center">freeupscale</h3>
<p align="center">Free image upscaler that runs in your browser. 2x and 4x, no sign-up, no watermark, no upload.</p>

Upscale anime art, drawings and photos with the open waifu2x models, right on your device with WebGPU (or a lighter
CPU engine). Your image never leaves your computer or phone. Available in 21 languages.

The upscaling models and the engine code it adapts are [waifu2x / nunif](https://github.com/nagadomi/nunif) by
nagadomi, under the MIT license. freeupscale is an independent project, not affiliated with nagadomi. The
[feedback](https://github.com/freeupscale/feedback) repository is the public issue tracker.
